While you wait for your permanent crown, a temporary crown protects the tooth. It is held on with a weak cement so that it can be removed easily, so avoid sticky foods such as caramel and chewing gum, and floss by sliding the floss out sideways rather than pulling it up.

If the temporary crown comes off, keep it and call us. You can usually put it back with a little over the counter dental cement, but we will fit it properly at an appointment soon after.

After the permanent crown is cemented, your gums may be tender and the tooth sensitive to temperature for a few days. If your bite feels high, tell us so we can adjust it. Clean the crown like any other tooth, with careful flossing around the edge.

## Short answer
With a temporary crown, avoid sticky foods and floss gently. Call us if it comes off, and tell us if the permanent crown feels high.
