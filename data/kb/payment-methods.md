We accept credit and debit cards, cash and bank transfers. Payment is due at the time of service for your share of the cost, after any insurance estimate. We give you a receipt and an invoice you can also download from the patient portal.

You can pay a balance online from the portal. In this demonstration environment, online card payments use a sandbox, which means the details are checked for format but no real money moves and no card details are stored.

Checks are not accepted at this time. If you want to pay by bank transfer, ask the front desk for the details and use your invoice number as the reference so that we can match your payment to your account.

## Short answer
We accept cards, cash and bank transfers, and you can pay online from the portal. In this demonstration the online card payment is a sandbox, so no real card is charged.
