Bringing a few items helps your first visit go smoothly. Please bring a photo identification, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines and supplements you take, including doses. Knowing about your medicines helps your dentist plan safe treatment.

If you have recent dental X-rays or records from another dentist, bring copies or ask your previous office to send them to us. This can save you from repeating X-rays. Please also note any allergies, including to latex or to numbing medicine, and any medical conditions such as heart disease, diabetes or pregnancy.

If you use a nightguard, retainer or dentures, bring them along. A comfortable list of questions is welcome too. There are no silly questions.

## Short answer
Bring photo identification, your insurance card, a list of your medicines and allergies, and any recent dental records or X-rays. Bring retainers, nightguards or dentures if you use them.
