Cavities form when bacteria in plaque turn sugar into acid, which slowly dissolves enamel. You can prevent most of them. Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth daily and visit us for regular cleanings and exams.

The number of times your teeth meet sugar matters more than the amount. Sipping sugary drinks or snacking through the day keeps your teeth under constant attack, so try to keep sweet foods to mealtimes and drink water between them. Sugar free gum can help your mouth produce protective saliva.

Fluoride makes enamel more resistant to acid. Professional fluoride treatments and sealants give extra protection to people with a high risk of decay. Tell us if you have a dry mouth, because it raises the risk.

## Short answer
Prevent cavities with fluoride toothpaste twice a day, daily cleaning between teeth, less frequent sugar and regular check-ups. Ask about sealants and fluoride treatments if you are at higher risk.
