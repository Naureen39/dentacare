Before your first visit we ask you to complete a short registration form, a medical and dental history and a consent form covering privacy and appointment reminders. You can fill these out online from the patient portal at any time, which usually saves about fifteen minutes at the front desk.

If you prefer paper, download the printable forms from the New Patients page or collect them at reception. Please arrive ten minutes early if you plan to complete them in the waiting room.

Your answers are confidential and stored securely. Giving a full health history helps your dentist keep you safe, so include any conditions, operations, allergies and medicines. If you are unsure about something, write what you know and the dentist will go over it with you.

## Short answer
New patients complete a registration form, a health history and a consent form. You can do this online beforehand or on paper at reception, arriving ten minutes early.
