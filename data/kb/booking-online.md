You can book online at any time. Choose a service, pick a dentist or select the first available time, and then choose a day and a time from the calendar. When you pick a time we hold it for five minutes while you enter your details.

New guests enter their name, email address and phone number, and tick the box to accept our privacy policy and appointment reminders. We email a six digit code to confirm your email address, and once you enter it your appointment is booked. You receive a confirmation with a calendar file attached.

If you have an account, sign in first and your details are filled in for you. You can book up to 90 days ahead, and same day times appear when they are open. If you get stuck, call {{clinic_phone}}.

## Short answer
Choose a service, a dentist and a time online, then confirm your email with a six digit code. Your chosen time is held for five minutes while you finish, and a confirmation email follows.
