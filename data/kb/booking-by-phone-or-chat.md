If you would rather talk to someone, call {{clinic_phone}} during opening hours. Our front desk can find the best time, check which services your insurance is likely to cover and answer questions about preparing for your visit.

You can also use the Meridian Assistant, the chat window on our website. It can show you open times, help you choose a service and book an appointment in a few taps. It answers common questions about hours, prices and policies using our own information, and it can pass you to a person when you would like one.

The assistant cannot give medical advice or diagnose problems. If you are in pain or worried about a symptom, please book a visit or call us so a dentist can look at it.

## Short answer
Call {{clinic_phone}} to book with a person, or use the Meridian Assistant on our website to book in a few taps. The assistant cannot give medical advice, but it can hand you to the front desk.
