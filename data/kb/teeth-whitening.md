Professional whitening lightens the natural color of your teeth by several shades. After a cleaning, your gums are protected with a barrier, and a whitening gel is applied to the teeth and activated for short periods over about an hour.

Whitening is most effective on yellow stains caused by coffee, tea, wine and age. Grey or brown stains from medicines or injury can be harder to change. Fillings, crowns and veneers do not whiten, so the dentist plans around them.

Some people notice sensitivity for a day or two, which a desensitizing gel and a toothpaste for sensitive teeth can help. Results can last a year or more, depending on your habits. Not suitable during pregnancy or for children, so ask your dentist.

## Short answer
In-office whitening lightens teeth by several shades in about an hour, after a cleaning. Some sensitivity for a day or two is normal, and results last a year or more.
