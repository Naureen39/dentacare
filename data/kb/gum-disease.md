Gum disease begins when plaque builds up along the gum line. The first stage, gingivitis, causes red, swollen gums that bleed when you brush or floss. It can be reversed with good home care and a professional cleaning.

If it is not treated, it can progress to periodontitis, where the infection damages the bone that supports your teeth. Signs include bad breath that will not go away, receding gums, loose teeth and pus between the teeth and gums. Smoking, diabetes and some medicines increase the risk.

Treatment depends on the stage. It can mean more thorough cleaning, a deep cleaning below the gum line or, in advanced cases, a visit to our periodontist. Regular exams catch gum disease early, when it is easiest to treat.

## Short answer
Gum disease starts with bleeding, swollen gums and can damage the bone around the teeth if untreated. Early gingivitis is reversible, so see us if your gums bleed.
