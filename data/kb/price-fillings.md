title: Price of a tooth colored filling
category: pricing
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
A tooth colored filling starts at $210 and takes about 45 minutes. The fee covers numbing, removing the decay and placing a resin filling matched to the shade of your tooth.

The final price depends on the size and position of the cavity. A small filling on the biting surface is simpler than a larger one that includes the sides of a back tooth, and larger fillings can cost more. Your dentist will tell you the fee before starting.

Most insurance plans cover fillings at a moderate percentage. If a tooth needs a filling, treating it early is usually cheaper than waiting, as a cavity that spreads can need a crown or a root canal treatment later.

## Short answer
A tooth colored filling starts at $210. The final price depends on the size and position of the cavity, and most insurance plans cover part of the cost.
