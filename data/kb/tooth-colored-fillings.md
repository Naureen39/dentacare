A filling repairs a tooth damaged by decay. After numbing the area, the dentist removes the decayed part of the tooth, cleans the space and fills it with a tooth colored resin. A special light hardens the resin in seconds, and the dentist shapes and polishes it so your bite feels natural.

Resin fillings bond to the tooth, so the dentist can remove less healthy tooth structure than older metal fillings required. They match the color of your teeth and blend in.

The visit takes about 45 minutes. The numbness lasts for a couple of hours, so avoid chewing until it wears off. A small filling is a very routine treatment, and finding and filling cavities early prevents more extensive treatment later.

## Short answer
A filling removes decay and replaces it with a tooth colored resin hardened by a light, in about 45 minutes. Treating cavities early avoids bigger problems.
