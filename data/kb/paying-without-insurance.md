You do not need insurance to be a patient at {{clinic_name}}. Our prices are the same for everyone, and we publish starting prices for common services so you can plan ahead. Self pay patients are asked to pay at the time of the visit.

Ask the front desk about our clear aligner and implant payment plans, which let you spread larger costs over several months. We can give you a written estimate before any treatment begins, so you always know what to expect.

If you have limited funds, tell your dentist. There is often a way to order treatment, putting the most urgent care first, so that you can look after your health and still stay within your budget.

## Short answer
You can be a patient without insurance. Self pay patients pay at the visit, and we give a written estimate first and can arrange payment plans for larger treatment.
