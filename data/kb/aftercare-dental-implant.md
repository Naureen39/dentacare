title: Aftercare after dental implant surgery

After an implant is placed, expect some swelling, minor bleeding and bruising for a few days. Use a cold pack on the cheek, keep your head raised when resting and eat soft foods. Avoid touching the area with your tongue or fingers and do not smoke, because smoking greatly raises the risk of implant failure.

Do not rinse hard on the first day, then rinse gently with warm salt water or a prescribed rinse after meals. Brush the rest of your mouth as usual, and clean around the implant with a soft brush as shown by the team.

The implant needs several months to fuse with the bone, and you will have follow-up visits to check progress. Call us for increasing pain, swelling or bleeding after the third day, a fever, or if the temporary tooth or healing cap comes loose.

## Short answer
Use a cold pack, eat soft foods, rinse gently and never smoke after implant surgery. Call us for worsening pain, swelling or bleeding after day three, or a fever.
