We recommend a child's first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months of the first tooth appearing. Early visits are short and friendly. The dentist counts the teeth, checks the gums and jaw, and gives you advice on cleaning, feeding and teething.

Prepare your child by talking about the visit in a positive way and reading a picture book about the dentist. Avoid words that suggest pain. Our pediatric dentist explains each step in simple words and shows the tools, so nothing is a surprise. Children can bring a favorite toy.

Parents may stay in the room, and we encourage a parent to sit in the chair for very young children. Bring your insurance details and any questions. A relaxed first visit builds comfort that lasts a lifetime.

## Short answer
Bring your child by their first birthday or soon after the first tooth appears. The visit is short and gentle, and parents can stay in the room.
