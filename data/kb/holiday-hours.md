We close on major public holidays, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On the days around some holidays we may close early, and our last week of December runs on a lighter schedule.

Planned closures are shown on the booking page: closed days simply have no available times. If you already have an appointment on a day we later need to close, we contact you as early as possible to move it to a time that suits you, at no charge.

If you have a dental emergency while we are closed, call {{clinic_phone}} and follow the recorded instructions. For severe swelling, bleeding that will not stop, or trouble breathing, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

## Short answer
We are closed on major public holidays and keep reduced hours in the last week of December. Closed days show no available times online, and emergencies outside opening hours should start with a call to {{clinic_phone}}.
