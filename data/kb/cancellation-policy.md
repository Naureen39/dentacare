We ask for at least 24 hours of notice if you need to cancel an appointment. A cancellation made more than 24 hours before the start time is free, and the time is released so another patient can use it.

If you cancel less than 24 hours ahead, it is recorded as a late cancellation on your account. Repeated late cancellations may mean we ask for a card on file when you book in future. Genuine emergencies such as illness or a family crisis are always treated with understanding, so please call us and explain.

You can cancel yourself in the patient portal, by using the cancel link in your confirmation or reminder email, or by calling {{clinic_phone}}. The portal and email both show the exact time until which cancellation is free.

## Short answer
Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment and it is free. Later cancellations are noted as late cancellations, and you can cancel in the portal, from the email link or by phone.
