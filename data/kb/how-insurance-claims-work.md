After your visit we send the claim to your insurance company for you. The insurer reviews it and pays its share to us, usually within two to six weeks. You pay the remaining part, such as a deductible, a copayment or any service your plan does not cover.

At the front desk we can often estimate your share on the day, and some patients pay it then. If the insurer pays less than expected, we send you an updated invoice rather than a surprise, and you can see all payments on your invoice in the portal.

Insurance is a contract between you and your insurer, so coverage limits and waiting periods apply to you even when we submit the claim. If a claim is denied, we help you understand why and appeal when it is reasonable.

## Short answer
We file claims for you. The insurer pays its share to us, usually within two to six weeks, and you pay the rest. Your invoice in the portal shows every payment.
