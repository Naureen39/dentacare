After root canal therapy, the area around the tooth can feel tender for a few days because the tissues around the root were inflamed. This is normal and usually mild. Ask your dentist or pharmacist which pain relief is suitable for you.

The tooth is sealed with a temporary filling until a crown is placed, and a treated tooth can be fragile. Avoid chewing hard or sticky foods on that side until the permanent restoration is done. Keep your follow-up appointment to have the crown fitted, because delay can allow the tooth to crack or the seal to leak.

Brush and floss normally. Contact us if you have severe pain or swelling, a reaction to the medicine, your bite feels uneven or the temporary filling comes out.

## Short answer
Mild tenderness for a few days is normal. Avoid hard foods on that side and keep your appointment for the crown. Call us for severe pain, swelling or a lost temporary filling.
