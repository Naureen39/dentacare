An orthodontic consultation starts at $75. It includes an exam, photographs or scans and a discussion of whether clear aligners or another approach would suit you. You leave with a plan, an estimated length of treatment and a quote.

Clear aligner treatment is priced as a plan fee and starts at $4,800. That fee covers the full series of custom aligners, scheduled check-ups with your orthodontist and a retainer at the end. Treatment commonly takes from six to eighteen months.

Many dental plans pay a lifetime orthodontic allowance, so check yours. We offer monthly payment plans, and the fee can often be paid from a flexible spending account. Children and teenagers are welcome, and we are happy to assess readiness early.

## Short answer
An orthodontic consultation starts at $75 and a clear aligner plan starts at $4,800 for the full treatment. Payment plans are available and some insurers pay an orthodontic allowance.
