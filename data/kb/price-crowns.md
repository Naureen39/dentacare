A porcelain crown starts at $1,150 per tooth. The fee includes preparing the tooth, a digital impression, a temporary crown while the final one is made, and placing the finished crown on a second visit.

The price can vary with the material and with any build up needed to support the crown. Your dentist will explain the options. A crown is usually recommended for a tooth that is cracked, badly decayed or has had a root canal, because it protects the tooth from breaking.

Many insurance plans cover part of the cost of a crown, but they often limit it to one tooth per year or ask for a waiting period. We can request pre-authorization so that you know the plan's share before we start.

## Short answer
A porcelain crown starts at $1,150 per tooth, including the temporary crown and the final fitting. Insurance often pays part of the cost, and we can request pre-authorization.
