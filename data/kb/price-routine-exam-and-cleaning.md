A routine exam and cleaning starts at $120 and takes about 45 minutes. It includes a check of your teeth and gums, a professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, and polishing. The dentist or hygienist also reviews your home care and answers questions.

Most dental plans cover routine exams and cleanings at a high percentage, often twice a year, so many insured patients pay little or nothing for them. If you need X-rays at the same visit, they may be billed separately.

A comprehensive exam with X-rays for new patients starts at $145. Regular cleanings every six months are the simplest way to keep treatment costs down, because problems found early are almost always smaller and cheaper to fix.

## Short answer
A routine exam and cleaning starts at $120. A comprehensive exam with X-rays for new patients starts at $145, and many insurance plans cover routine cleanings.
