Many people feel anxious about dental visits, and you are not alone. Tell us when you book, or at the start of your visit. Our team never judges, and knowing how you feel lets us adapt the visit to you.

There are many ways we can help. We explain each step before it happens, agree on a hand signal that means stop and take breaks whenever you need one. We offer numbing gel, headphones, blankets and a screen to watch. A morning appointment can help if you worry through the day.

For some people, a longer first visit that only involves talking and a gentle look is a good way to start. If these steps are not enough, sedation options are available. Your comfort comes first.

## Short answer
Tell us if you are nervous and we will slow down, explain each step and let you signal for breaks. Headphones, numbing gel and sedation options are available.
