A crown is a cap that covers a whole tooth, restoring its shape, strength and appearance. It is recommended when a tooth is cracked, heavily filled, badly worn or has had a root canal.

On the first visit, the dentist numbs the tooth and shapes it to make room for the crown. A digital scan captures its exact shape, and a temporary crown protects the tooth while the permanent one is made. On the second visit the temporary crown is removed and the porcelain crown is checked for fit, color and bite before it is cemented.

With good care, a crown can last many years. Brush and floss around it like a natural tooth, and avoid biting down on very hard items such as ice or hard candy.

## Short answer
A crown covers and protects a damaged tooth. It takes two visits: one to prepare the tooth and place a temporary crown, and one to fit the finished porcelain crown.
