You are entitled to a copy of your dental records, including X-rays, and we are glad to provide them. Ask at the front desk, call {{clinic_phone}} or send a request to {{clinic_email}} from the address on your account, so we can confirm it is you.

For your security we need a signed request before releasing records, and we can send them directly to another dentist or to you. Digital copies are provided by a secure link rather than ordinary email attachments.

Records are usually ready within five business days. There is no charge for sending records to another dental office when you transfer care. If you want to correct something in your record, tell us and the dentist will review it.
