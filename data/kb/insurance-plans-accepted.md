We work with most major types of dental insurance. These include preferred provider organization plans, health maintenance style plans, employer group plans and many individual plans. Because plan details differ so much, we describe the types we accept rather than listing brand names.

Out of network patients are welcome too. Many plans reimburse part of the cost of visits outside their network, and we will provide the paperwork you need to claim it.

The best way to confirm your coverage is to bring your insurance card and member number to your first visit, or send them ahead through the portal. We will check your benefits and give you an estimate before treatment. Call {{clinic_phone}} if you would like us to look at your plan before you book.

## Short answer
We accept most preferred provider, health maintenance style and employer group plans, and welcome out of network patients. Bring your insurance card and we will check your benefits before treatment.
