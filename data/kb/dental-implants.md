A dental implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up. A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over several months. A connector and a custom crown are then added, and the result looks and works like a natural tooth.

Implants are placed with local anesthetic, and sedation is available. The post placement takes around two hours. Healing takes roughly three to six months before the final crown is fitted, and you will have temporary solutions during that time if needed.

Good candidates have healthy gums and enough bone, and do not smoke. A consultation, with a scan, shows whether an implant is right for you. With careful brushing, flossing and regular check-ups, implants can last for decades.

## Short answer
A dental implant is a titanium root placed in the jaw that supports a custom crown. It takes several months to heal, and a consultation with a scan shows if you are a good candidate.
