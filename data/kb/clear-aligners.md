Clear aligners are custom made, nearly invisible trays that gradually straighten your teeth. Each tray is worn for about two weeks before you move to the next one, which is slightly different in shape and guides your teeth into place.

Treatment starts with a consultation and a digital scan, from which the orthodontist plans every step and shows you a preview of the expected result. You wear the trays around 22 hours a day, taking them out to eat, drink anything but water, brush and floss.

Check-ups every six to eight weeks make sure your teeth move as planned. Treatment typically lasts six to eighteen months, followed by a retainer to hold the new position. Aligners suit many, though not all, bite problems.

## Short answer
