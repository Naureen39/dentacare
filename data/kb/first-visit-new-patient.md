Welcome to {{clinic_name}}. A first visit usually takes about an hour. After a warm welcome at reception, we confirm your details and review your medical and dental history. A member of the team then takes any X-rays your dentist needs and the dentist performs a thorough exam of your teeth, gums, bite and mouth.

You will leave with a clear picture of your oral health. If you need treatment, your dentist explains the options, the expected cost and how insurance may help, and you decide what suits you. Many new patients also have a cleaning at the same visit when time allows.

Please arrive ten minutes early with your photo identification and insurance card. If you feel nervous, tell us. We go at your pace.

## Short answer
A first visit takes about an hour and includes a history review, X-rays if needed and a full exam. Your dentist explains any treatment and cost before anything is done.
