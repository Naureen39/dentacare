updated_at: 2026-09-15

Your lips, tongue and cheek may stay numb for two to four hours after a filling. Avoid chewing on that side until the feeling has fully returned, and take care not to bite your cheek or tongue while numb. Drinking hot liquids is best postponed too, because you may not feel the temperature.

It is common for the tooth to be sensitive to hot, cold and pressure for a few days to a couple of weeks. This usually settles by itself. A toothpaste for sensitive teeth can help.

If your bite feels too high, so that your teeth meet unevenly, call us because a quick adjustment fixes it. Contact us too if the pain gets worse, lasts beyond two weeks, or the filling feels rough or loose. Brush and floss as usual, gently around the new filling.

## Short answer
Avoid chewing until the numbness wears off. Some sensitivity for a few days is normal, but call us if your bite feels high or the pain gets worse.
