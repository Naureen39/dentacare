We use modern equipment to make visits faster, more precise and more comfortable. Digital X-rays show a clear image on screen within seconds and use less radiation than traditional film. Intraoral cameras let you see what the dentist sees, which makes it easier to understand your treatment options.

Many of our restorations are planned with digital impressions, so most patients can skip the gooey trays. Our treatment rooms have adjustable chairs, and we offer blankets, headphones and numbing gel to help you relax.

We keep our equipment serviced and updated on a regular schedule. If you are curious about a tool or a technique, ask. Your dentist is happy to explain what each piece of equipment does and why it is being used.

## Short answer
We use digital X-rays, intraoral cameras and digital impressions to make care precise and comfortable. Digital X-rays use less radiation than film and show results immediately.
