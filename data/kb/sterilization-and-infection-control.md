Your safety comes first. Every reusable instrument is cleaned, packaged and sterilized in a heat sterilizer after each patient, and the sterilizers are tested regularly to confirm they work. Instruments stay sealed in their pouches until they are opened in front of you.

Surfaces in the treatment room are disinfected between patients, and disposable items such as gloves, masks, suction tips and bibs are used once and thrown away. Our team wears protective equipment appropriate to each procedure and washes their hands before and after every patient.

Water lines are flushed and maintained according to public health guidance. We follow recommendations from national dental and public health authorities and review our procedures with the whole team each year.

## Short answer
Instruments are sterilized after every patient, surfaces are disinfected between visits, and single use items are thrown away. We follow national infection control guidance and review our procedures every year.
