Your health information is private, and we protect it. We collect only what we need to care for you, to bill correctly and to contact you about appointments. Sensitive details such as date of birth, phone number, address and insurance member number are stored in encrypted form.

Only team members who need your information to do their job can see it, and every time staff open your record the access is logged. We do not sell your information or use it for third party advertising, and our emails contain no tracking pixels.

You may ask to see your records, correct mistakes or request a copy of what we hold. Our full Notice of Privacy Practices explains your rights in detail. Questions can be sent to {{clinic_email}}.

## Short answer
We collect only what we need, store sensitive details encrypted and log every staff access. We never sell your information, and you can ask for a copy or a correction at any time.
