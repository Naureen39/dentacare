title: What to do if a tooth is knocked out
category: emergency
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
A knocked-out adult tooth can often be saved if you act quickly. Pick the tooth up by the crown, the part that is normally visible, and avoid touching the root. If it is dirty, rinse it briefly with water or milk. Do not scrub it or remove tissue attached to it.

If you can, gently put the tooth back into its socket and hold it there by biting softly on a clean cloth. If that is not possible, keep it moist in a small container of milk, or in your own saliva. Do not store it in plain water.

Call {{clinic_phone}} immediately and come in within the hour if you can, because the chances of saving the tooth fall quickly. Baby teeth should not be put back, but still call us. Seek emergency care for any head injury.

## Short answer
Hold the tooth by the crown, rinse it gently, put it back in the socket or keep it in milk, and call {{clinic_phone}} right away. The first hour matters most.
