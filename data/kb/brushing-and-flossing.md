title: Brushing and flossing well
category: oral-health
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
Brush twice a day for two minutes with a soft toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Hold the brush at a slight angle to the gum line and use small, gentle circles, covering every surface of every tooth. Brushing hard does not clean better and can wear gums.

Clean between your teeth once a day with floss or small interdental brushes, since a brush cannot reach the sides of the teeth where plaque collects. Curve the floss around each tooth and slide it just under the gum edge.

Replace your toothbrush, or the head of an electric brush, every three to four months, or sooner if it looks worn. Spit out the toothpaste but do not rinse with water straight afterward, so the fluoride keeps working. A tongue scrape can help freshen your breath.

## Short answer
Brush twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste and clean between your teeth daily. Replace your brush every three to four months.
