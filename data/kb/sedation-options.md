Alongside local anesthetic, which numbs the area being treated, we offer sedation for patients who feel very anxious or need long or complex treatment. The main options are nitrous oxide and oral sedation.

Nitrous oxide, often called laughing gas, is breathed through a small mask. It helps you feel relaxed within minutes and wears off quickly after the mask is removed, so most people can drive home. Oral sedation is a prescribed tablet taken before the visit, which makes you drowsy but awake. You need someone to drive you and stay with you afterward.

We discuss your medical history, medicines and preferences before choosing an option, and monitor you throughout. Sedation is priced separately, and your dentist will explain the cost beforehand.

## Short answer
We offer nitrous oxide, which wears off quickly, and oral sedation, which needs an adult to take you home. Your dentist reviews your health first and the cost is quoted beforehand.
