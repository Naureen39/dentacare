You should never be surprised by a bill. Before any treatment beyond a routine visit, we prepare a written estimate that lists each procedure, its fee, the amount we expect your insurance to pay and what remains for you.

For larger treatment we can ask your insurance company for a pre-authorization, which is a written statement of what the plan intends to cover. It takes a few weeks to arrive and is an estimate rather than a guarantee, but it gives much better certainty.

Estimates stay valid for ninety days, and your dentist will go through them with you in person. Nothing proceeds until you have agreed. If the treatment plan changes, for example because the dentist finds more decay, we give you an updated estimate first.

## Short answer
We give a written estimate before treatment and can request pre-authorization from your insurer for larger work. Estimates stay valid for ninety days, and nothing proceeds until you agree.
