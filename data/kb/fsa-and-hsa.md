Flexible spending accounts and health savings accounts can usually be used for dental care. These accounts let you set aside pre-tax money for eligible health costs, and dental exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns and orthodontic care are generally eligible.

You can pay with the card linked to your account, or pay us and send the receipt to your plan administrator for reimbursement. We provide an itemized receipt for every visit, and you can download invoices from the patient portal at any time.

Cosmetic treatments such as teeth whitening are often not eligible, and rules differ between plans, so check with your account administrator when you are unsure. Many accounts have a use it by date, so schedule your visits before the deadline.

## Short answer
FSA and HSA funds can generally be used for exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns and orthodontics. Pay with your account card or send our itemized receipt for reimbursement.
