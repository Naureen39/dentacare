Start cleaning your baby's gums with a soft cloth before teeth appear, and brush the first teeth twice a day with a smear of fluoride toothpaste, about the size of a grain of rice. From age three, use a pea-sized amount. Help your child brush until they can tie their own shoelaces, around age six or seven.

Limit sugary drinks and snacks, and offer water between meals. Never put a baby to bed with a bottle of anything but water. Sipping sugary drinks all day is a common cause of cavities in young children.

Sealants, thin protective coatings on the chewing surfaces of back teeth, can prevent most cavities in the years after molars arrive. Fluoride treatments strengthen enamel. Regular checks every six months let us spot problems early.

## Short answer
Brush a child's teeth twice a day with a small amount of fluoride toothpaste, limit sugary drinks and ask us about sealants and fluoride. Check-ups every six months catch problems early.
