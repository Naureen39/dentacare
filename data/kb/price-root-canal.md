title: Price of root canal therapy
category: pricing
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
Root canal therapy starts at $980 and takes about ninety minutes. The fee covers numbing, cleaning out the infected tissue inside the tooth, shaping and sealing the canals and placing a temporary filling.

The price depends on the tooth. Front teeth have a single canal and are simpler, while back molars have several canals and need more time. Most root-treated teeth also need a crown afterward to protect them, which is priced separately.

Insurance usually covers root canal therapy at a similar rate to other major treatment. Keeping the natural tooth is often better for chewing and for the neighbouring teeth than extracting it, and it can cost less over time than replacing the tooth with an implant.

## Short answer
Root canal therapy starts at $980. A crown is usually needed afterward and is priced separately, and insurance typically covers part of the cost.
