The patient portal is your secure online space with the clinic. After you create an account and confirm your email address, you can see upcoming and past appointments, book, reschedule or cancel, and update your contact details and insurance information.

You can also view your invoices, download a PDF copy and pay a balance online. Payments in this demonstration are simulated in a sandbox, so no real card is charged. The portal keeps a list of the reminders we have sent you.

Your account is protected with a strong password, and you can turn on two step sign in with an authenticator app for extra security. Sessions end automatically after a period of inactivity. If you forget your password, use Forgot password on the sign in page.

## Short answer
The portal lets you manage appointments, view and pay invoices, download PDFs and update your details. Create an account with your email address and you can add two step sign in for extra security.
