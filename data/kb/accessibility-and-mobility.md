Our clinic is designed to be welcoming to everyone. The entrance is step free, the doors open automatically, and all treatment rooms and restrooms are reachable by wheelchair or walker.

If you use a mobility aid, let us know when you book so we can prepare a room with extra space. Dental chairs lower to make transfers easier, and our team is glad to help you get comfortable.

For patients who are deaf or hard of hearing, we can communicate in writing and slow down our explanations. If you would like an interpreter, please tell us a few days ahead so we can arrange one. Patients with sensory sensitivities can ask for a quieter room and extra time.

## Short answer
The clinic has a step free entrance and rooms that are accessible by wheelchair. Tell us about any mobility, hearing or sensory needs when you book and we will prepare for your visit.
