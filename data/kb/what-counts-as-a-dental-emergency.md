Some problems need care the same day. These include severe toothache that does not settle, a knocked-out or loose adult tooth, a broken tooth with pain, a swollen face or gums, a lost crown or filling with pain, and bleeding after a procedure that will not stop.

Call us at {{clinic_phone}} and describe what has happened. We keep time free every day for emergencies, and we will tell you how quickly to come in. A lighter problem, such as a chipped tooth that does not hurt, can usually wait for the next available appointment.

Go to the nearest emergency room or call 911 for swelling that spreads to the eye or neck, difficulty breathing or swallowing, severe bleeding or a serious injury to the face or jaw. Do not wait for the clinic to open.

## Short answer
Severe pain, a knocked-out tooth, facial swelling or uncontrolled bleeding need urgent care. Call {{clinic_phone}}, and for trouble breathing or swallowing or serious facial injury call 911.
