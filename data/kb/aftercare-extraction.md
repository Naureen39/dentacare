title: Aftercare after a tooth extraction
category: aftercare
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
After an extraction, a blood clot forms in the socket. This clot is essential for healing, so protect it. Bite gently on the gauze for 30 to 45 minutes. Do not rinse forcefully, spit, drink through a straw or smoke for at least 24 hours, since these can dislodge the clot and cause a painful dry socket.

Some swelling and mild bleeding are expected on the first day. A cold pack on the cheek, 15 minutes on and 15 minutes off, helps with swelling. Rest, keep your head raised and eat soft, cool foods such as yogurt or soup for a few days.

From the second day, rinse gently with warm salt water after meals. Keep brushing the rest of your teeth. Call us if bleeding does not slow, pain worsens after three days, or you have a fever or bad taste.

## Short answer
Protect the blood clot: no straws, forceful rinsing, spitting or smoking for 24 hours. Use a cold pack, eat soft foods and call us if pain gets worse after three days.
