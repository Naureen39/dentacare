We try to run on time, and we ask the same of our patients because each appointment is planned around the treatment you need. If you are running late, please call {{clinic_phone}} as soon as you know so we can tell you what is possible.

If you arrive up to ten minutes late, we will usually still see you, although the visit may be a little shorter. If you are more than fifteen minutes late, we may need to reschedule so that your treatment is not rushed and the patients after you are not delayed.

When we are running behind ourselves, we let you know and do our best to keep waits short. Our check in is quick if you complete your forms online first, which also helps if traffic makes you late.

## Short answer
Call us if you are running late. We can usually still see you if you arrive within ten minutes, but after about fifteen minutes we may need to reschedule.
