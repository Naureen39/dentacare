A comprehensive exam is the foundation of good care and is recommended for every new patient. It takes about an hour and includes a review of your history, a complete check of your teeth, gums, bite and jaw joints, and an oral cancer screening.

The dentist takes the X-rays needed to see between teeth, under old fillings and below the gum line, where problems often begin unnoticed. We will then show you what we found and explain what it means in plain language.

You receive a written summary and, if treatment is recommended, an estimate with the order we suggest. A comprehensive exam with X-rays starts at $145, and your insurance may cover part of it.

## Short answer
A comprehensive exam takes about an hour and includes a full check, an oral cancer screening and the X-rays your dentist needs. It starts at $145, and insurance may cover part of it.
