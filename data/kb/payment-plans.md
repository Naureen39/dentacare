Some treatments cost more than most of us budget for at once, so we offer payment plans for larger care such as dental implants, crowns and clear aligner treatment. A plan divides the amount you owe after insurance into equal monthly payments.

Plans for clear aligners are built around the treatment fee, which starts at $4,800 and covers all of your aligners. Many patients make a deposit at the start and pay the rest monthly until their treatment ends.

Ask for a plan at your treatment planning visit and the front desk will prepare a written schedule that shows each payment date and amount. There are no hidden fees, and you can pay a plan off early at any time.

## Short answer
We offer monthly payment plans for larger treatment such as implants, crowns and clear aligners. Ask at the front desk for a written schedule with no hidden fees.
