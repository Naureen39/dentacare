A missed appointment, where a patient does not arrive and has not cancelled, is recorded as a no show. Empty chairs mean other patients wait longer for care, so we take them seriously, but we also know that life gets in the way.

The first missed appointment is a reminder, and our team will contact you to reschedule. After repeated no shows we may ask you to confirm each appointment a day ahead or to provide a card to hold future bookings.

You can avoid missing a visit by responding to our reminders. We email you two days and one day before, with a link to confirm or cancel in a single click. If you are unwell or something has come up, simply let us know.

## Short answer
A missed appointment without notice is recorded as a no show. We will help you rebook, and repeated no shows may mean we ask you to confirm each visit in advance.
