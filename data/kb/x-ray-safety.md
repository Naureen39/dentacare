title: Are dental X-rays safe?
category: new-patients
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
Dental X-rays use a very small amount of radiation. A typical set of bitewing X-rays gives a dose similar to what a person receives from natural background sources in a day or two. Digital sensors, which we use, need even less than film.

X-rays are taken only when they help your dentist make a decision, and how often depends on your age, history and risk of decay. A lead apron and thyroid collar are available and used whenever they are recommended.

Tell us if you are pregnant or think you might be. In most cases the dentist will wait until after the pregnancy for routine X-rays, but if you have an urgent problem the safest option can be to treat it.

## Short answer
Dental X-rays use a very small radiation dose, and digital X-rays use less than film. We take them only when they help your dentist, and you should tell us if you are pregnant.
