We publish starting prices for our most common services so you can plan before you book. A starting price is the fee for a typical, uncomplicated case. Your dentist confirms the exact fee after examining you, because the number of teeth, the materials and the difficulty of the case can change it.

The fees listed here are before insurance. If you have a plan, your insurer may pay part of the cost, and we show the estimate for your share before treatment starts. Prices are the same for insured and self pay patients.

You can see current starting prices on our Pricing page and ask the Meridian Assistant about any service. Prices are reviewed once a year, and we tell patients with planned treatment before a change takes effect.

## Short answer
We list starting prices for common services, before insurance. The dentist confirms the exact fee after your exam and we give a written estimate before any treatment.
