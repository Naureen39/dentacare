A dental implant is a titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Placement of an implant starts at $3,200 and takes about two hours. The crown that goes on top of the implant is priced separately and follows after the implant has healed.

Before placing an implant we recommend an implant consultation, which starts at $90 and includes an exam and a review of your X-rays or scan. If you need a bone graft or a sinus lift to prepare the site, that is an additional fee.

Insurance coverage for implants varies widely and is often limited, so we request pre-authorization where possible. We also offer monthly payment plans so that the cost can be spread over time.

## Short answer
Implant placement starts at $3,200, with a consultation from $90 and the crown priced separately. Payment plans are available and insurance coverage varies by plan.
