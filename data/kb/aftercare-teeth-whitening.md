title: Aftercare after teeth whitening

updated_at: 2026-09-15

For the first 48 hours after whitening, your teeth are more likely to pick up stain, so avoid coffee, tea, red wine, dark sauces, berries and tobacco. Choose lighter colored foods such as chicken, rice, pasta and white sauces instead, and brush after meals.

Some sensitivity to hot and cold is common for a day or two. Use a toothpaste made for sensitive teeth and avoid very hot and cold drinks. Contact us if sensitivity lasts longer than a few days.

To keep your result, brush twice a day, floss daily and keep up with regular cleanings. A touch-up treatment after a year or so can refresh your smile. Remember that fillings and crowns do not change color, so they may look slightly different from your whitened teeth.

## Short answer
For 48 hours avoid dark foods, coffee, tea, wine and tobacco. Mild sensitivity is common and passes within a day or two.
