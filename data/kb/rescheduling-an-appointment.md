title: How to reschedule an appointment
category: policies
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
Plans change, and moving an appointment is easy. Sign in to the patient portal, open your upcoming appointments and choose Reschedule. You pick a new day and time from the live calendar, and your original time is released automatically once the new one is confirmed.

You can also call {{clinic_phone}} and the front desk will move it for you. If you only have your confirmation email, use the cancel link and then book a new time online.

The same 24 hour notice applies when you reschedule: moving an appointment earlier than that is treated like a late cancellation. After you reschedule you receive a fresh confirmation email with an updated calendar file.

## Short answer
Reschedule in the patient portal, which shows live open times, or call {{clinic_phone}}. Give at least 24 hours of notice to avoid a late cancellation record.
