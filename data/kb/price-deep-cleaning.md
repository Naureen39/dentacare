A deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, is priced per quadrant of the mouth and starts at $220 per quadrant. Each visit takes about an hour and treats one quadrant, though some patients have two quadrants done together.

A full mouth has four quadrants, so treating all of them starts at $880 in total. Your dentist will tell you how many quadrants need treatment after measuring the gum pockets around each tooth. Many people need only one or two.

Insurance often covers deep cleaning when your dentist documents gum disease, usually at a lower percentage than a routine cleaning. We will check your benefits and give an estimate before we begin. Local anesthetic is used so the treatment is comfortable.

## Short answer
A deep cleaning starts at $220 per quadrant, so a full mouth starts at $880. Insurance often covers part of it when gum disease is documented.
