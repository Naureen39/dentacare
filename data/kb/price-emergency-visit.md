An emergency visit starts at $160. This covers a focused exam, any X-ray needed to find the cause of the problem and treatment to relieve pain, such as dressing a broken tooth or opening a tooth to drain infection.

If the problem needs further treatment, such as a root canal, a filling or an extraction, that is quoted separately and can often be done at the same visit if time allows. We always explain the choices and the cost first.

Most insurance plans cover emergency exams. We keep time free each day for emergencies, so you will often be seen the same day. Call {{clinic_phone}} as early as you can, and tell us what has happened so we can prepare.

## Short answer
An emergency visit starts at $160 and covers an exam, an X-ray if needed and treatment to relieve pain. Further treatment is quoted separately, and we aim to see emergencies the same day.
