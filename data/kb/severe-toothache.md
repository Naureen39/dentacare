title: What to do for a severe toothache
category: emergency
updated_at: 2026-09-15
---
A severe toothache needs a dentist, because the pain is a sign that something is wrong inside or around the tooth. While you arrange to be seen, rinse your mouth with warm salt water and gently floss to remove any food caught between the teeth.

A cold pack on the outside of the cheek can ease pain and swelling. Do not place aspirin or any medicine directly on the gum, since this can burn the tissue. Ask your dentist or pharmacist what pain relief is safe for you.

Call us as early as you can at {{clinic_phone}} and tell us about your symptoms, because we reserve time for emergencies each day. If you also have a fever, facial swelling or difficulty breathing or swallowing, go to an emergency room straight away, since these can signal a spreading infection.

## Short answer
Rinse with warm salt water, floss gently, use a cold pack and call {{clinic_phone}} for a same day visit. Go to the emergency room if you have a fever with facial swelling or trouble breathing.
