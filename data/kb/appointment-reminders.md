When you book, we email a confirmation straight away with the details and a calendar file you can add to your phone or computer. Two days before your visit we send a reminder with a button to confirm your appointment or cancel it, and a second reminder follows the day before.

You can respond with one click from the email. Confirming helps us know you are coming, and cancelling releases the time for another patient. If you confirm early, we skip the first reminder.

After your visit, we send a short thank you with aftercare contacts, and about six months later we send a gentle recall note when it is time for your next check-up. Reminders go to the email address on your account, so keep it up to date.

## Short answer
We email a confirmation when you book, reminders two days and one day before, a thank you after your visit and a recall note about six months later. Reminder emails include one click confirm and cancel links.
