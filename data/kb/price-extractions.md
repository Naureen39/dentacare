A simple extraction, where the tooth is visible and can be removed without cutting, starts at $240. A surgical extraction, which is used for broken teeth or impacted wisdom teeth and may require a small incision, starts at $480.

Both fees include numbing and a brief set of aftercare instructions. Extra sedation, if you choose it for comfort, is discussed beforehand and priced separately.

After an extraction you might want to replace the tooth, with an implant, a bridge or a partial denture, to keep your bite stable. We discuss replacement options at the same visit and give a written estimate. Most insurance plans cover part of the cost of extractions.

## Short answer
A simple extraction starts at $240 and a surgical extraction starts at $480. Replacement options, such as an implant, are priced separately and explained at the visit.
