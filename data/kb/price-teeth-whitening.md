Professional teeth whitening starts at $420 for an in-office session of about an hour. It uses a stronger whitening gel than over the counter products and is carried out under supervision, with your gums protected, so it is fast and gentle.

Because whitening is a cosmetic treatment, dental insurance does not cover it. It is also often not eligible for flexible spending accounts, so check with your plan administrator if you are unsure.

Results vary with the type and cause of the staining. Before whitening we recommend a check-up and cleaning, since fillings and crowns do not change color and cavities should be treated first. Your dentist can estimate how much lighter your smile will be.

## Short answer
In-office teeth whitening starts at $420 for about an hour. It is cosmetic, so insurance does not cover it, and a check-up first is recommended.
