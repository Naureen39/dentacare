Free patient parking is available in the lot beside the building, with spaces close to the entrance for people who find walking difficult. There is also accessible parking directly outside the main doors.

On busy mornings the lot can fill up. If it is full, there is additional street parking on the surrounding blocks and a public garage within a short walk. Please check posted signs for time limits on the street.

Parking is free for the length of your visit. If you need a few extra minutes for a longer procedure, tell the front desk at check in and we will make sure you are not ticketed in our lot.

If you are unsure where to park, call {{clinic_phone}} before you arrive and we will point you to the nearest open space.

## Short answer
Free patient parking is in the lot beside the building, with accessible spaces by the main doors. Street parking and a public garage are nearby if the lot is full.
